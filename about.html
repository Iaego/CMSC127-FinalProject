Our Story

Three generations of family-run Baguio hospitality since 1968.

Heritage · Est. 1968

From a six-room inn to Baguio's beloved family hotel

In 1968, newlyweds Vicente and Christian Barrozo opened a six-room inn along Leonard Wood Road. Vicente hand-crafted the beds from salvaged Benguet pine while Christian served warm breakfast from the La Trinidad market.

They named the inn Vicentenium, meaning "Vicente's place, built to last a century." Rebuilt stronger after 1990 and now led by granddaughter Lourdes Barrozo-Cayme, our 42-room hotel blends heritage warmth with easy online reservations.

Our Mission

To give every guest a restful, genuinely Baguio experience through heartfelt service, local flavors, and honest room rates.

Our Vision

To remain the Cordilleras' most trusted family-run hotel, welcoming generations of returning guests while caring for Baguio's pines.

Founder's Promise

"A hotel is not just walls and beds, it is the hot cup of coffee waiting for a tired traveller." - Vicente Barrozo (1936-2015)
